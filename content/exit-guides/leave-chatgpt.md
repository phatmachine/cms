## hero.title
Leave ChatGPT

## hero.standfirst
Since February it has carried advertising, aimed using the content of your conversations. You tell it things, and it decides what to <em>sell</em> you.

## subject.statement
People confide in it the way they once confided in a diary, or a doctor. It is neither. It is an advertising surface with a sympathetic voice, and a <em>record</em> that courts have already reached into.

## subject.model
Ads And Subscriptions

## reason.01.title
The Conversation Is The Target

## reason.01.body
Ads went live in February 2026 and OpenAI opened self-serve ad buying in July. Targeting works from the context of what you type, not keywords, so the confiding is the mechanism.

## reason.02.title
Deleted Was Not Deleted

## reason.02.body
A court ordered OpenAI to preserve every chat, including deleted ones, from May to September 2025. Those logs still exist, lawyers are reading them, and the list of flagged accounts can still grow.

## reason.03.title
Owned, Not Independent

## reason.03.body
The non-profit foundation controls it, but Microsoft holds around 27 per cent and access to the models until 2032. Your conversations sit inside an American company with an American shareholder.

## alternatives.intro
Two European assistants. One cannot read what you store, the other is simply <em>better</em> at the work.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Proton Lumo

## alternative.01.locationLine
AI Assistant · Geneva

## alternative.01.blurb
A Swiss assistant that stores your history with zero-access encryption, keeps no server-side logs and never trains on what you type. Its apps are open source, so the claim can be <em>checked</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It is not end-to-end encrypted in use, because the model has to read your prompt to answer it — the encryption protects what is stored, not what is processed. It is also plainly weaker than the frontier models.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Switzerland, with processing in European data centres under GDPR. Outside US legal process.

## alternative.01.ownership
Proton AG, Geneva, controlled since June 2024 by the non-profit Proton Foundation. Same owner as the mail service in issue 001.

## alternative.01.funding
Paid subscriptions. No ads, no training on user data, no investors to answer to.

## alternative.01.price
Free tier with daily limits, usable without an account. Lumo Plus from about €9.99 a month billed yearly.

## alternative.02.label
The Capable European

## alternative.02.name
Mistral Le Chat

## alternative.02.locationLine
AI Assistant · Paris

## alternative.02.blurb
A French lab whose models sit close to the frontier, hosted in Europe under GDPR, with much of its model work released openly. This is the option you reach for when the work actually has to be <em>good</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
It is a heavily funded company racing the American labs, which is a different thing from a privacy company. Check the training settings on the free tier rather than assuming your conversations are excluded.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
France. EU jurisdiction and Paris-hosted infrastructure are its stated differentiator.

## alternative.02.ownership
Mistral AI SAS, Paris, founded 2023 by Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample and Timothée Lacroix. Venture funded.

## alternative.02.funding
Subscriptions, enterprise contracts and API sales, on top of large venture rounds. No advertising.

## alternative.02.price
Free consumer tier. Pro is around $14.99 a month per person.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Read Your Own History

## exitRoute.01.body
Before anything else, scroll back through what you have told it. Most people have forgotten, and the forgetting is exactly the problem worth seeing.

## exitRoute.02.title
Export, Then Prune

## exitRoute.02.body
Request the data export from Settings and keep it offline. Then delete the conversations you would not want read aloud by somebody else's lawyer.

## exitRoute.03.title
Clear The Memory

## exitRoute.03.body
Memory is separate from chat history and survives deleting conversations. Empty it explicitly, or the profile it built on you stays behind.

## exitRoute.04.title
Move One Real Task

## exitRoute.04.body
Take a job you actually do weekly and run it somewhere else for a fortnight. Habit, not principle, is what keeps people here.

## exitRoute.05.title
Close The Account

## exitRoute.05.body
Delete from Settings, Data controls, and expect roughly thirty days. Note that anything preserved under the 2025 court order is outside your reach.
