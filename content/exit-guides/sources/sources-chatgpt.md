## Dropped for lack of source

- **"OpenAI keeps your deleted chats forever."** This is the most widely repeated claim about ChatGPT privacy and it is now false. The preservation order was terminated on 9 October 2025, with the obligation ending 26 September 2025. Printing the "forever" version would have been the single easiest error to make on this page. Reason 02 instead states the true and narrower thing: a four-month window of everyone's deleted conversations was preserved, still exists, and is being read.
- **"Your conversations train the model by default."** OpenAI's training settings differ by tier and have changed repeatedly. Not verified this session, so not printed.
- **Ad targeting using your stored memory or cross-session profile.** OpenAI describes context-based targeting within a session. Whether persistent memory feeds targeting is not documented in anything found here. The page says only what OpenAI describes.
- **Mistral's shareholder register.** A large 2025 round reportedly made an industrial investor Mistral's biggest shareholder. Not verified this session, so the ownership field says "venture funded" without naming parties. Worth resolving — it matters for the people-over-profit test.
- **Whether Mistral trains on free-tier Le Chat conversations.** Widely assumed, not confirmed here. The catch tells the reader to check the setting rather than asserting either way.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **`subject.users` = 800 Million.** Sources this session give 700M, 800M and 900M+ within eight months, and none is a filed disclosure. Pick one OpenAI statement with a date and cite that, or the number will be wrong whichever way it moves.
- **Both alternative prices.** Lumo's monthly figure appears as $12.99, $13.39 and €9.99 across sources; Le Chat Pro at $14.99 comes from a pricing aggregator. Confirm both on the companies' own pages. This is the third issue running where aggregator pricing has disagreed with itself.
- **The January 2026 log delivery to NYT.** Load-bearing for how immediate reason 02 feels. Confirm against the court docket.
- **Proton's ownership** is carried over from issue 001 rather than re-checked. Fine for now; re-verify when issue 001 is next refreshed, since both pages would fail together.

## Goes stale

- **The ad rollout.** Fastest-moving item here. Ads reached seven countries by July 2026 with stated plans to expand, and the minimum spend has already been removed once. Reason 01's geography will be out of date within months. Re-check quarterly.
- **NYT v. OpenAI.** Still live. The flagged-user list can grow, and further orders could change reason 02 in either direction. Re-check quarterly.
- **OpenAI's corporate structure.** Restructured in October 2025 with an IPO openly discussed. If it lists, the ownership field and reason 03 both change.
- **Lumo's model quality.** The catch says it is weaker than frontier models, which is true now and is exactly the kind of claim that ages badly. Re-check at each republication.
- **Mistral's independence.** A frontier lab burning capital is an acquisition or a pivot away from a different page.
