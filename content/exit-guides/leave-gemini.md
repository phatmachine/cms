## hero.standfirst
Google's own privacy notice asks you not to type anything confidential, because a human being may <em>read</em> it. Take the warning at face value.

## subject.statement
No other product opens by telling you to hold something back. Google has written down exactly what Gemini is, in its own notice, and almost nobody has <em>read</em> it.

## reason.01.title
They Told You Not To

## reason.01.body
Google's notice states that human reviewers, including contractors, read some of what you type. It then asks you not to enter anything you would not want a reviewer to see.

## reason.02.title
Off Is Not Off

## reason.02.body
Turning Keep Activity off does not stop the reading. Google's own page says your chats are still used, still reach human reviewers, and are still held for around seventy-two hours.

## reason.03.title
Reviewed Means Kept

## reason.03.body
Conversations pulled for human review are stored separately from your account and held for up to three years. Deleting your activity does not delete them, because they are no longer filed under you.

## alternatives.intro
One keeps the work in Europe, under laws that actually bite. The other never lets it leave the <em>room</em> at all.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Mistral Le Chat

## alternative.01.locationLine
AI Assistant · Paris

## alternative.01.blurb
A French lab whose models sit near the frontier, hosted in Europe under GDPR, with much of its model work published openly. It is the swap that costs you the least <em>capability</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
This is a well-funded company racing the American labs, not a privacy company, and the two are not the same thing. Check its training settings yourself rather than assuming your conversations are excluded.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
France. EU jurisdiction and Paris-hosted infrastructure are its stated differentiator.

## alternative.01.ownership
Mistral AI SAS, Paris, founded 2023 by Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample and Timothée Lacroix. Venture funded.

## alternative.01.funding
Subscriptions, enterprise contracts and API sales on top of large venture rounds. No advertising.

## alternative.01.price
Free consumer tier. Pro is around $14.99 a month per person.

## alternative.02.label
The Nothing-Leaves Route

## alternative.02.name
Jan

## alternative.02.locationLine
Local AI · Your Machine

## alternative.02.blurb
An open-source desktop app that downloads a model once and then runs it entirely on your own computer, offline. There is no reviewer, no retention policy and no server, because there is no <em>server</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
4 — Involved

## alternative.02.catch
A model small enough to run on your laptop is noticeably weaker than Gemini, and an older machine will be slow. You are also now the one responsible for updates and backups.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
None that applies in use. Inference happens on your hardware, so there is no company holding your conversations.

## alternative.02.ownership
Maintained by Menlo Research and released under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with the full source on GitHub.

## alternative.02.funding
Open-source project. Free to download, with telemetry off by default and remote providers opt-in.

## alternative.02.price
Free. The real cost is a computer capable of running the model.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Read The Notice

## exitRoute.01.body
Open the Gemini Apps Privacy Notice and find the line about confidential information. Five minutes of reading does more than any argument on this page.

## exitRoute.02.title
Empty The History

## exitRoute.02.body
Go to myactivity.google.com, delete your Gemini activity and turn Keep Activity off. Anything already pulled for human review stays, and that is the point.

## exitRoute.03.title
Take It Off The Phone

## exitRoute.03.body
On Android, Gemini has replaced the old assistant, so check what holds the home button now. Revoke its access to messages, calls and connected apps.

## exitRoute.04.title
Check The Other Doors

## exitRoute.04.body
Gemini also sits inside Gmail, Docs and Search under separate settings. Turning it off in one place leaves the others running.

## exitRoute.05.title
Move One Real Task

## exitRoute.05.body
Run a job you actually do each week somewhere else for a fortnight. Convenience is what keeps people here, so it has to be beaten on its own ground.
