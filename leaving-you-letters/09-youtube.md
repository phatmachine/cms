# Leaving You — YouTube

Status: Approved by Mark with his edit to the "I'm done" paragraph, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't teach me things — you taught me almost everything. How to tie a tie. How to fix a bike. How to pass the exam I'd skipped the classes for.

You raised me. You were the babysitter when everyone was busy, the friend after school, the voice in the dark when I couldn't sleep. You know every cartoon I watched at six, every gamer I idolised at twelve, every rabbit hole I fell into at fifteen.

I thought you were showing me the world. I didn't understand you were choosing which world I saw.

And you knew I was a child. You told toy companies you were the number one website kids visited, while acting as though children's privacy law didn't apply to you. The US Federal Trade Commission said you tracked children to sell them ads without asking their parents, and you paid what was then a record fine to settle.

Then the videos got shorter. Endless. Swipe, swipe, swipe. I learned American slang before I learned my grandmother's songs. The stories of where I'm from got quieter while the feed got louder.

And you were never just YouTube. You're Google: the same company behind Search, Gmail, Android and Gemini.

I'm done letting an algorithm decide who I become. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done swiping my evenings away.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Video I choose, not video that chooses me. Creators paid directly by the people who watch them. Stories from my own place, told by my own people.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
