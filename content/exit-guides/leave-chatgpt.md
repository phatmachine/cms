## reason.01.title
I Said Too Much

## reason.01.body
Ads went live in February 2026 and OpenAI opened self-serve ad buying in July. Targeting works from the context of what you type rather than from keywords, so the confiding is the mechanism, and that is what changed it for me.

## reason.02.title
Deleted Wasn't Deleted

## reason.02.body
A court ordered OpenAI to preserve every chat, including deleted ones, from May to September 2025. Knowing those logs still exist and that lawyers are reading them is enough to stop me typing.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Read Your Own History

## exitRoute.01.body
Before anything else, scroll back through what you have told it. Most people have forgotten, and the forgetting is exactly the problem worth seeing.

## exitRoute.02.title
Export, Then Prune

## exitRoute.02.body
Request the data export from Settings and keep it offline. Then delete the conversations you would not want read aloud by somebody else's lawyer.

## exitRoute.03.title
Clear The Memory

## exitRoute.03.body
Memory is separate from chat history and survives deleting conversations. Empty it explicitly, or the profile it built on you stays behind.

## exitRoute.04.title
Move One Real Task

## exitRoute.04.body
Take a job you actually do weekly and run it somewhere else for a fortnight. Habit, not principle, is what keeps people here.

## exitRoute.05.title
Close The Account

## exitRoute.05.body
Delete from Settings, Data controls, and expect roughly thirty days. Note that anything preserved under the 2025 court order is outside your reach.
