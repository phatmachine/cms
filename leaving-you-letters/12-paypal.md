# Leaving You — PayPal

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you weren't convenient — you were. One login, and I could pay anyone, anywhere.

You know what I buy, who I pay, who pays me, what causes I give to and how much. You know my rent, my side hustle, and the friend I lent money to and never asked for back.

I thought you were my wallet. I didn't understand you held the keys, not me.

People found out the hard way. Accounts frozen. Money held. A policy reason that explained nothing. You cut off a free-speech campaign group on the right and independent news sites on the left. Some got their accounts back after a public outcry; others didn't. Then you published a rule that would let you take a penalty straight from people's balances for spreading "misinformation." When people noticed, you called it an error and pulled it.

And while you held my money, you shared what I bought. You changed your settings so the shops I use could learn from my purchases — switched on, unless I found the switch.

You were never just PayPal. You're Venmo, Xoom and Honey too.

I let you stand between me and every payment I make. But I'm done asking permission to use my own money. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting you decide whose money is acceptable.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Credit unions owned by their members. Payments that move my money without reading the receipt. Accounts protected by banking law, not a user agreement.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
