## reason.01.title
Opted In Without Asking

## reason.01.body
In July 2024 X switched users into sharing their posts for Grok training by default. An opt-out arrived later, buried in settings, after the collecting had already happened.

## reason.02.title
The Model Keeps It

## reason.02.body
Ireland's regulator forced X to stop and to delete the European data it had taken. X was not required to delete the models already trained on it, so the learning survives the deletion.

## reason.03.title
The Owner Changed

## reason.03.body
SpaceX bought xAI in February 2026 and dissolved it entirely by July. Whatever you weighed up when you started using Grok, you are now dealing with a different company.

## alternatives.intro
The honest field here is small. One assistant cannot read what you store, and the other never <em>sends</em> it anywhere.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Proton Lumo

## alternative.01.locationLine
AI Assistant · Geneva

## alternative.01.blurb
A Swiss assistant that stores your history with zero-access encryption, keeps no server-side logs, and never trains on what you type. The apps are open source, so the promise can be <em>checked</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It is not end-to-end encrypted while working, because the model has to read your prompt to answer — encryption protects what is stored, not what is processed. It is also weaker than the frontier models.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Switzerland, processing in European data centres under GDPR. Outside US legal process.

## alternative.01.ownership
Proton AG, Geneva, controlled since June 2024 by the non-profit Proton Foundation. Same owner as the mail service in issue 001.

## alternative.01.funding
Paid subscriptions. No ads, no training on user data, no investors to satisfy.

## alternative.01.price
Free tier with daily limits, usable without an account. Lumo Plus from about €9.99 a month billed yearly.

## alternative.02.label
The Nothing-Leaves Route

## alternative.02.name
Jan

## alternative.02.locationLine
Local AI · Your Machine

## alternative.02.blurb
An open-source desktop app that downloads a model once, then runs it on your own computer with the network off. No training policy can reach you, because nothing ever <em>leaves</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
4 — Involved

## alternative.02.catch
A model small enough for a laptop is clearly weaker than Grok, and older machines will struggle. Updates and backups become your job rather than somebody else's.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
None applies in use. Inference happens on your hardware, so no company is holding your conversations.

## alternative.02.ownership
Maintained by Menlo Research and released under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with the full source published on GitHub.

## alternative.02.funding
Open-source project. Free to download, telemetry off by default, remote providers opt-in.

## alternative.02.price
Free. The real cost is a computer capable of running the model.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Find The Toggle

## exitRoute.01.body
In X, open Settings, then Privacy and safety, then Grok. Turn off the permission to use your posts and interactions for training.

## exitRoute.02.title
Clear The Conversations

## exitRoute.02.body
Delete your Grok chat history separately from your X account. The two are stored apart, and clearing one leaves the other untouched.

## exitRoute.03.title
Request The Archive

## exitRoute.03.body
Ask X for your full data archive before you change anything else. It takes days to arrive and you will want it regardless of how far you go.

## exitRoute.04.title
Cancel The Subscription

## exitRoute.04.body
If you pay for Premium or SuperGrok, cancel it directly rather than just deleting the app. Billing outlives uninstalling, every time.

## exitRoute.05.title
Move One Real Task

## exitRoute.05.body
Take the thing you actually use it for and do it elsewhere for a fortnight. For most people that is checking claims, which a search engine still does.
