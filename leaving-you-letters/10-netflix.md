# Leaving You — Netflix

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you're bad at stories — you're very good at them.

You know what I watch when I'm sad, what I watch when I can't sleep, which shows I started and never finished, and the exact moment I usually give up. You know I said "one more episode" and meant four.

I came for the stories. You built a machine to keep me there. The next episode starting before the credits finish. Whole seasons released at once. Trailers playing the moment I paused to choose. Your own founder said your biggest competitor was sleep. I thought he was joking.

And while I watched, the stories from home got harder to find. When Canada asked streaming services to put part of what they earn there back into Canadian film and television, you went to court to fight it. I can find dozens of American shows in a minute. I have to go searching for one made where I live.

I let you become my evenings, my weekends, my company. But I'm done trading my sleep for your next episode. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting one company's feed decide what my culture looks like.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Films I choose, then switch off. Local broadcasters and storytellers making work where I live. Libraries — free, and they don't autoplay.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
