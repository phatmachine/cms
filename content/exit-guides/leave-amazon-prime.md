## hero.standfirst
Amazon's own staff named the cancellation process after Homer's <em>Iliad</em>. A court agreed it was designed that way, and Amazon paid two and a half billion dollars.

## subject.statement
Every subscription is a bet that you will forget to cancel. Prime is the only one where the difficulty was <em>engineered</em>, documented internally, and then proven in a federal court.

## reason.01.title
Named For An Epic

## reason.01.body
Amazon staff called the cancellation flow "Iliad", after the poem about a ten-year journey home. The name was internal, documented, and it tells you exactly what the design was meant to achieve.

## reason.02.title
A Court Agreed

## reason.02.body
In September 2025 a federal judge found Amazon took billing details before disclosing what Prime cost and how to cancel. Amazon settled three days into trial for two and a half billion dollars.

## reason.03.title
You Paid, Ads Came

## reason.03.body
In 2024 Amazon put adverts into Prime Video and charged extra to remove them, rising again in April to five dollars a month. A court called that a benefit modification rather than a price rise.

## alternatives.intro
Prime is a bundle, and the delivery half has no honest replacement. What follows covers the part most people actually <em>use</em>.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
MUBI

## alternative.01.locationLine
Curated Film · London

## alternative.01.blurb
A hand-picked rotating selection of independent and international cinema, with no advertising and no algorithm pushing you toward whatever is cheapest to licence. It also puts films into actual <em>cinemas</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
The catalogue is deliberately small and rotates, so the blockbuster you wanted will not be there. It is also venture backed at a billion-dollar valuation, with a Sequoia-led round and a board seat held by a Chinese-backed investor.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
United Kingdom. MUBI Limited operates from London under UK and EU consumer law.

## alternative.01.ownership
MUBI Limited, London, founded 2007 by Efe Çakarel. Venture backed; investors include Sequoia Capital, Summit Partners and Closer Media.

## alternative.01.funding
Subscriptions, plus film distribution and production. No advertising anywhere in the product.

## alternative.01.price
Around $14.99 a month or $119.88 a year, and £11.99 a month in the UK.

## alternative.02.label
If You Keep Video

## alternative.02.name
PeerTube

## alternative.02.locationLine
Video Hosting · France

## alternative.02.blurb
French non-profit video hosting, where a channel lives on a server you pick or one you run yourself. For home footage and anything you made, this keeps it <em>outside</em> a retailer's account.

## alternative.02.difficulty
4 — Involved

## alternative.02.catch
This is not a film service and never pretended to be, so it replaces nothing you watch for entertainment. It is here for what you store, not what you stream.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
France for the project and its steward. Instances fall under the law where each is hosted.

## alternative.02.ownership
Created and maintained by Framasoft, a French non-profit association. Released under the AGPL.

## alternative.02.funding
Donations and public interest grants. No advertising, no investors, nothing to sell.

## alternative.02.price
Free to join an instance, and free to run your own.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Check For Your Refund

## exitRoute.01.body
If you held Prime between June 2019 and June 2025, you may be owed up to fifty-one dollars. Some refunds were automatic; a claims process opened afterwards.

## exitRoute.02.title
Count What You Use

## exitRoute.02.body
List the Prime benefits you touched in the last year. Most people find two, against a hundred and thirty-nine dollars, and the list ends the argument.

## exitRoute.03.title
Rescue The Photos

## exitRoute.03.body
Prime includes unlimited full-resolution photo storage, and losing it is the thing people regret. Download everything before you cancel, not after.

## exitRoute.04.title
Cancel, And Expect Friction

## exitRoute.04.body
The flow is legally required to be simpler now. Go to Manage Membership and refuse every offer to pause, downgrade or take a discount instead.

## exitRoute.05.title
Let The Delivery Habit Die

## exitRoute.05.body
Without Prime, the minimum order threshold makes you batch purchases and wait. That pause is doing the real work, and it is worth more than the fee.
