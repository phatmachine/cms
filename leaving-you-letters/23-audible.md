# Leaving You — Audible

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't keep me company — you did. On long drives, sleepless nights, the walk home in the dark.

You know every book I've finished and the ones I abandoned at chapter three. The voices I fall asleep to. The credits I save up, the sales I wait for, the series I've listened to twice.

I thought I was building a library, shelf after shelf, paid for one credit at a time.

Then I read the fine print. I didn't buy those books. I bought a licence to listen to them, locked inside your app, playable only where you allow, and only while you keep the rights. I can't lend one to a friend. I can't leave them to my children.

Your parent company has already shown me what that means. It once reached into people's e-readers and deleted copies of a book they had paid for. The book was *1984*.

And now even the voices are changing. You've started offering publishers AI narration: machine voices reading stories that human voices used to carry.

You were never just a bookshop. You're Amazon: the same company behind Kindle, Prime and Alexa.

But I'm done paying full price to borrow. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done keeping my library in a building someone else can lock.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Audiobooks sold without locks, as files I keep. Libraries that lend for free and pay authors. Shops whose audiobook sales support the bookshop down the road.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
