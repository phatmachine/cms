# Leaving You — Apple

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you aren't beautiful — you are. You're the most beautiful thing I own.

You hold my photos from the day my daughter was born, every message I've sent in a decade, my health records, my heartbeat on my wrist, the card I tap to pay, the key to my car. Everything talks to everything. It just works.

I came for the design. I stayed because leaving got harder every year. My messages turned green when I talked to friends outside. My photos only felt at home on your devices. My apps were bought in your shop and lived nowhere else.

The garden is beautiful. But the walls are high, and they were built on purpose. The US Department of Justice is suing you, saying you illegally monopolised the smartphone market. Europe fined you for stopping app makers from telling me about cheaper deals outside your store. And when a US court ordered you to open the gate, a federal judge found you'd deliberately put new obstacles in the way, and that one of your executives lied under oath about it.

You were never just a phone. You're Apple: the shop, the bank card, the cloud, the music, the TV, all behind the same wall.

But I'm done paying extra for the privilege of being locked in. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done mistaking a beautiful wall for a home.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Phones that let me install what I choose. Files in formats any device can open. Services that work the same whatever brand my friends use.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
