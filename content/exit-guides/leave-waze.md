## hero.title
I'm Leaving Waze

## hero.standfirst
Waze may collect your location even when you are not using the app, and Google has owned it since 2013. Leaving takes an <em>afternoon</em>.

## subject.statement
Waze is a driving diary with a map attached. It saves a route history of every journey, and its policy lets Google's other map products use a de-identified <em>copy</em>.

## subject.jurisdiction
ISRAEL / USA

## subject.owner
Google (Alphabet Inc.)

## subject.users

## subject.model
Location Ads

## reason.01.title
Every Journey Saved

## reason.01.body
Waze's policy says that, depending on your settings, it may collect your location "even when you are not using the app", and it saves a route history of every journey. I found no retention period for it, and I would rather not keep that diary.

## reason.02.title
Google Owns The Map

## reason.02.body
Waze's policy says it shares de-identified account settings, location and route data with other Google mapping and navigation products. Google bought Waze in 2013 and folded its team into Google Maps in 2022, so I no longer see a separate company.

## reason.03.title
Two Countries' Laws

## reason.03.body
Waze's terms are governed solely by Israeli law, with disputes heard in Tel Aviv, and its owner is American. Its policy says it will assist law enforcement where it believes the law requires, so my driving sits under two legal systems I did not choose.

## alternatives.intro
One keeps live traffic; the other gives it up and says it collects nothing. Both draw on OpenStreetMap, a map <em>built</em> by volunteers rather than owned by a company.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Magic Earth

## alternative.01.locationLine
Navigation · Amsterdam

## alternative.01.country
Netherlands

## alternative.01.blurb
A navigation app built on OpenStreetMap that adds live traffic and speed cameras, so it is the nearest thing to Waze on this page. It says it does not track you, does not profile you and does not trade in your <em>data</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
Its source code is closed, so its privacy claims cannot be checked from outside, and its own policy says position data may be sent to a traffic provider, anonymised and kept for five minutes. Live traffic needs the paid plan.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Netherlands, under EU data protection law. Magic Lane is based in Amsterdam, with development in Romania.

## alternative.01.ownership
Magic Lane International B.V., a privately held company in Amsterdam. No public shareholder list was found.

## alternative.01.funding
A small subscription and sales of its navigation software kit to other companies. It says it carries no advertising.

## alternative.01.price
Free to start. Live traffic needs a subscription: €0.99 a year in Magic Earth's 2025 announcement, but the US App Store lists a premium plan at $19.99, so check before you buy.

## alternative.01.referralUrl
https://www.magicearth.com/

## alternative.02.label
The Open-Source Route

## alternative.02.name
CoMaps

## alternative.02.locationLine
Offline Maps · Community-Run

## alternative.02.blurb
A free, open-source offline map and navigation app, run by volunteers as a not-for-profit fork of Organic Maps. It states that it has no advertising and no personal <em>data</em> collection.

## alternative.02.difficulty
3 — Moderate

## alternative.02.catch
There is no live traffic and no community reports, which is the thing Waze does best, so you will plan around jams yourself. It is also a young project, first released in June 2025 and still formalising its legal structure.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
No company of its own yet. It raises funds through a fiscal host on Open Collective and publishes its code on Codeberg.

## alternative.02.ownership
No owning company. A community fork of Organic Maps under open governance, with a not-for-profit structure still being formalised.

## alternative.02.funding
Community funding through Open Collective, in line with its not-for-profit principles.

## alternative.02.price
Free.

## alternative.02.referralUrl
https://www.comaps.app/

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Install Magic Earth

## exitRoute.01.body
Install Magic Earth, download your country's maps and save Home and Work by hand. Drive a familiar route first, before you trust it on a new one.

## exitRoute.02.title
Export Your History

## exitRoute.02.body
Sign in at waze.com/account, choose Download your Waze data and request the archive. It holds your drive history and searches, so you can see what Waze kept.

## exitRoute.03.title
Move The Dashboard

## exitRoute.03.body
Waze has no subscription to cancel, so start with the car. Set Magic Earth as your navigation app in CarPlay or Android Auto and drop Waze from the screen.

## exitRoute.04.title
Keep A Net

## exitRoute.04.body
For a fortnight, keep Waze but set its location access to only while using the app, so it cannot follow you in the background. Open it only if the new app lets you down.

## exitRoute.05.title
Delete For Good

## exitRoute.05.body
Check the archive opens first. Then delete the account from your profile, under Account and login. Waze says it cannot recover a deleted account, so there is no undo.
