# Leaving You — WhatsApp

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you broke the lock — you didn't. My messages are still encrypted, and that matters.

You hold my family group chat, my best friend's voice notes at 3 a.m., the first photos of my newborn niece, the last message my grandmother ever sent me. You were the one app everyone I love already had.

I chose you because you were simple. Just messages. No feed, no games, no noise. Just us.

Then one day, someone else was in the room. A glowing ring in my chats. An assistant in my search bar, ready to talk to me like a friend. You called it optional — but I couldn't switch it off. Italy's competition regulator opened an investigation into whether you put your AI into WhatsApp without asking users first.

And you were never just WhatsApp. You're Meta: the company behind Facebook, Instagram and Messenger, now building an AI that wants a seat in every conversation I have.

I trusted you with the most private conversations of my life. Leaving feels like moving my whole family to a new house. But I'm done sharing my chats with a machine that pretends to be company. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done making room for something I never invited.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Messengers that are only messengers. Apps run by non-profits, not ad companies. Group chats with only the humans in them.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
