## Dropped for lack of source

- **The 2023 privacy policy change collecting biometric data and employment history.** Widely reported at the time and a strong reason if still current. Not verified against X's live policy this session, and three years old. Chase it for a future revision.
- **"X is an illegal monopoly" or similar.** No such finding exists. The December 2025 decision is about transparency and deceptive design, not market power.
- **Advertiser exodus and revenue collapse figures.** Numerous estimates circulate, most from ad-industry sources with an interest. None verified, and the page does not need them.
- **Content moderation and hate speech volumes.** Genuinely contested, heavily studied, and — per the Commission's own finding — impossible for outsiders to measure properly because researcher access is blocked. That irony is the substance of reason 02, so the page makes the access point rather than a claim about content it cannot verify.
- **The itemised €45m/€35m/€40m split of the fine.** Single source. Total only is printed.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **Everything about the fine should be cited to the Commission's own press release and decision**, not to law-firm summaries. The facts are consistent across eight independent sources, which is strong, but this is a regulatory decision about a litigious company and the primary document is public. Get it.
- **Bluesky's funding history.** The Series A is solid; the $97m Series B and the Bain-led $700m round trace to a blog aggregator and a Mastodon thread respectively. Either verify or reduce the catch to "well over a hundred million dollars", which the Series A plus seed rounds already supports.
- **X's appeal status.** X was given 60 and 90 working days to respond, and Musk publicly attacked the decision. Whether X has appealed, complied or been penalised further is not established here. Check before publishing — if the fine is under appeal, the page should say so.

## Goes stale

- **DSA enforcement against X.** Other investigations remain open beyond the three findings fined. Further decisions are likely, and reason 01 or 02 may need updating within a quarter.
- **Mastodon's restructuring.** The Belgian AISBL has been "coming" since January 2025. If it completes, the ownership field and catch both improve and should be rewritten promptly — it is the kind of good news this series should report.
- **Bluesky's monetisation.** The catch says it has no revenue model. That is the single most likely thing on this page to change, and when it does the whole slot-two recommendation needs rethinking.
- **Bluesky's leadership.** A founder-led PBC that just lost its founding CEO, with a successor search running. Re-check at republication.
- **X's user count.** The 550 million figure is a March 2026 snapshot from an IPO filing. Third-party estimates run roughly half that. Do not swap in a weaker number later.
