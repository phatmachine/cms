# Leaving You — Amazon Prime

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you weren't generous — free delivery, films and music, all in one membership.

You know how often I order, how fast I want it, which shows I fall asleep to. You know I stopped comparing prices, because why would I, when the delivery was already paid for?

I don't remember really deciding to join you. Somewhere between "add to basket" and "buy now," a button said yes before I noticed.

And when I tried to go, you turned leaving into a journey. Page after page. Offer after warning. Are-you-sure after are-you-sure. Your own staff named that way out the "Iliad," after an epic about a war that lasted ten years. The US Federal Trade Commission said you deceived people into joining and then trapped them there. You paid billions to settle, and promised to make leaving simple.

You're Amazon's front door. Prime is how one company became my shop, my cinema, my music and my delivery van all at once.

I let you become a habit I paid for every month. But I'm done paying a membership fee to be loyal. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done searching for the exit.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Subscriptions I can cancel in one click. Delivery I pay for only when I need it. Services that let me leave as easily as I arrived.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
