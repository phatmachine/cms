## reason.01.title
It Told Them Wrong

## reason.01.body
Throughout a four-year inquiry TikTok denied that European data was stored in China, then reported in April 2025 that some had been. Ireland's regulator fined it five hundred and thirty million euros.

## reason.02.title
The Landlord Changed

## reason.02.body
January's American joint venture rearranged who owns the US business, not what the app collects. Everywhere outside the United States, ByteDance still owns the whole thing.

## reason.03.title
Sovereign Either Way

## reason.03.body
Fifteen per cent of the American venture sits with an Abu Dhabi investment firm, and Oracle now hosts the data and retrains the feed. The question was never whether a state could reach it.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Download Everything

## exitRoute.01.body
Request your data from Settings, Privacy, Download your data. Ask for all of it, including the videos, and wait for the file before going further.

## exitRoute.02.title
Save The Videos Properly

## exitRoute.02.body
Export your own uploads at full quality rather than screen-recording them. Years of work should not survive only as somebody else's compressed copy.

## exitRoute.03.title
Turn Off Personalisation

## exitRoute.03.body
In Settings, switch off ad personalisation and activity-based recommendations. Do this even if you are staying, because it costs you nothing.

## exitRoute.04.title
Tell People Where

## exitRoute.04.body
Post one video naming where you have gone and pin it to your profile. Then stop feeding the feed, because posting keeps you findable here instead.

## exitRoute.05.title
Delete, Not Just Uninstall

## exitRoute.05.body
Deleting the app leaves the account and its history live. Delete the account itself in Settings, and note the thirty-day window before it becomes final.
