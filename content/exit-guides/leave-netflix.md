## hero.title
Leave Netflix

## hero.standfirst
It stopped publishing how many subscribers it has, and from next year it will report what people watch once instead of <em>twice</em>. Growing companies rarely say less.

## subject.statement
Every change since 2022 has pointed one way: towards advertising, and away from telling anyone what is happening. You are not being <em>watched</em> here so much as counted, quietly.

## reason.01.title
They Stopped Counting

## reason.01.body
Netflix ended quarterly subscriber reporting in 2025 and will cut its viewership reports from twice a year to once from 2027. A company that discloses less as it grows is telling you something.

## reason.02.title
Worth More With Ads

## reason.02.body
Netflix built its own advertising technology and an ad-supported viewer is worth several times an ad-free one. Every incentive inside the company now points toward the version with commercials.

## reason.03.title
The Cheap Door Closed

## reason.03.body
The cheaper advert-free tier was shut and the remaining one raised to almost eighteen dollars. Paying less now means accepting advertising, which was not the arrangement anyone signed up to.

## alternatives.intro
Neither has the box sets, and no honest page will claim otherwise. One is free and <em>public</em>; the other belongs to the people who make it.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
ARTE

## alternative.01.locationLine
Public Broadcasting · Strasbourg

## alternative.01.blurb
A Franco-German cultural channel streaming free with no advertising and no account required, roughly nine tenths of it European production. Ninety-one million Europeans reach it each <em>month</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
This is documentaries, European cinema, concerts and arts programming, not the next returning drama everyone is discussing. Much of the catalogue is also time-limited, appearing for a window and then going.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
France and Germany. A European public broadcaster under European law, with no commercial parent.

## alternative.01.ownership
ARTE GEIE, a European Economic Interest Grouping owned jointly by ARTE France and ARTE Deutschland. Not listed and not for sale.

## alternative.01.funding
Public broadcasting funds from France and Germany. No advertising, no subscriptions, no investors.

## alternative.01.price
Free. No account needed to watch, and nothing to cancel later.

## alternative.02.label
The Creator-Owned Route

## alternative.02.name
Nebula

## alternative.02.locationLine
Streaming · New York

## alternative.02.blurb
The largest creator-owned streaming service, where the people making the programmes hold the company and receive the subscription money directly. No advertising and no sponsor <em>reads</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
The library is documentaries, essays and explainers from video makers, so it replaces evening viewing rather than films. CuriosityStream also holds a minority stake, so creator ownership is the majority rather than the whole.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
United States. Inside US legal process, unlike the first option.

## alternative.02.ownership
Nebula Entertainment and Broadcasting LLC, owned by its creators through Standard, with CuriosityStream holding a minority stake.

## alternative.02.funding
Subscriptions only, shared with the creators whose work you watch. No advertising anywhere.

## alternative.02.price
Around $6 a month, or $60 a year, often discounted through a creator's link.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Look At The List

## exitRoute.01.body
Open your viewing history and read the last three months. Most people find they paid for a handful of evenings, which settles the question quickly.

## exitRoute.02.title
Finish What You Started

## exitRoute.02.body
Note the one series you genuinely want to complete and finish it this month. Leaving mid-season is what pulls people back within a fortnight.

## exitRoute.03.title
Try The Free One First

## exitRoute.03.body
Watch something on a public broadcaster before cancelling anything. Free and good is a surprise worth having while you still have the alternative running.

## exitRoute.04.title
Cancel, Do Not Pause

## exitRoute.04.body
Netflix will offer to pause or downgrade you to advertising instead. Both keep the account alive, which is exactly the point of the offer.

## exitRoute.05.title
Buy One Film

## exitRoute.05.body
Spend what a month cost on a single film you own outright. It is the cheapest possible demonstration of what renting has been costing you.
