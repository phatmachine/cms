## Dropped for lack of source

- **The Grok content scandals.** Through 2025 and 2026 Grok generated repeated controversies over non-consensual sexualised imagery, the "MechaHitler" episode and image-manipulation abuse. Widely reported and almost certainly the reason many readers want out. Not printed, for two reasons: none was verified against a primary record this session, and a page built on offensive outputs argues about taste rather than data. The three reasons used are structural and documented. **If this series ever runs a content-safety angle, it needs its own research pass and probably its own format.**
- **SpaceX's government and defence contracts.** A genuinely strong sovereignty angle — the counterparty holding your conversations also holds US government contracts — but not researched here. Do not assert it without sources.
- **"Grok has no privacy policy of its own."** Not checked. xAI's policies were folded into SpaceX's during 2026 and the current position is unverified.
- **The $0.42-per-organisation US government contract for Grok.** Appears in a statistics aggregator. Striking if true, unverified, and not needed by the page.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **"buried in settings" in reason 01.** Sourced to a secondary guide rather than observed. Either open X's settings and confirm where the toggle sits, or soften the wording. Everything else on this page is documented.
- **Both alternatives are carried forward rather than re-verified,** including Lumo's pricing, which already conflicted across sources in issue 006. If the price-verification pass suggested earlier happens, this page inherits the fix.
- **The corporate history is sourced to trade and secondary coverage,** not to SEC filings directly. The facts are consistent across at least six independent sources, which is unusually strong, but for a claim this central it is worth citing the S-1 and the merger announcement directly.

## Series note — the alternatives pool

This is the third AI guide and the pool of genuinely qualifying assistants is small: Lumo, Le Chat and a local-model route are close to the whole honest field. Pairings have been varied across issues 006–008 rather than repeated wholesale, but the same names recur, and that is a real finding rather than a research failure. Two options if the repetition grates:

- Accept it and say so openly on the archive page. The field is small because the business model is hard.
- Research a genuinely different fourth option for future issues. Candidates not yet examined: Kagi Assistant, DuckDuckGo AI Chat, Venice.ai, Nextcloud Assistant, Aleph Alpha.
