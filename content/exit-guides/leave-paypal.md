## hero.title
Leave PayPal

## hero.standfirst
Three American states have laws forcing PayPal to ask permission before sharing what you buy. Everywhere else, it simply <em>started</em>.

## subject.statement
A payment processor knows what you bought, where, and how often. PayPal decided that record was an advertising asset, and the only thing that stopped it was <em>law</em>.

## subject.jurisdiction
USA

## subject.owner
PayPal Holdings, Inc.

## subject.users

## subject.model
Fees And Ads

## reason.01.title
Opted In By Default

## reason.01.body
PayPal's own privacy statement says that unless the law requires your consent, it discloses what you buy to partners and merchants for personalised shopping. The setting exists, but you had to go looking.

## reason.02.title
Three States Got Asked

## reason.02.body
In California, North Dakota and Vermont, PayPal shares only if you tell it to, because those states require it. Same company, same data, and the difference is entirely whether a legislature insisted.

## reason.03.title
Opting Out Is Partial

## reason.03.body
Switch the setting off and PayPal still discloses whatever it considers necessary to complete your transactions. Once that reaches a merchant, their privacy policy governs it, not yours.

## alternatives.intro
One works almost everywhere and is honest about its fees. The other is owned by European banks rather than <em>investors</em>.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Wise

## alternative.01.locationLine
Payments · London

## alternative.01.blurb
Cross-border payments with the exchange rate and fee shown before you commit, averaging about half a per cent. Nineteen million customers, and no advertising business built on what you <em>spend</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
There is no buyer protection scheme, so it does not replace PayPal for buying from strangers online. It also moved its primary listing to Nasdaq in May 2026, which puts a European fintech under American market structures.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
United Kingdom, FCA regulated, with licences across the EU and beyond. Now listed in New York as well as London.

## alternative.01.ownership
Wise Group plc, publicly listed. Founder-led, with Kristo Käärmann as chief executive since 2011.

## alternative.01.funding
Transaction fees, card spending and interest on balances. No advertising and no data sold to marketers.

## alternative.01.price
Free to hold an account. Transfers cost around 0.52 per cent on average, shown upfront.

## alternative.02.label
The European Route

## alternative.02.name
Wero

## alternative.02.locationLine
Bank Payments · Brussels

## alternative.02.blurb
A wallet built by a coalition of twenty European banks, moving money directly between accounts with no card network in the middle. More than forty-three million people already <em>use</em> it.

## alternative.02.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
It only works if your bank is in it, which for now means France, Germany, Belgium, and Luxembourg and the Netherlands as they arrive. Online shop payments are only now rolling out, so it is not yet a full PayPal substitute.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
European Union. EPI Company SE is a European Company, operating inside the single euro payments area.

## alternative.02.ownership
EPI Company SE, owned by twenty major European banks and payment providers rather than outside shareholders.

## alternative.02.funding
Bank-funded infrastructure, paid for by the members. No advertising and no data business.

## alternative.02.price
Free between individuals, through your existing banking app.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Turn The Sharing Off

## exitRoute.01.body
Open Data and Privacy, then Manage shared info, and disable Personalised shopping. In the UK and EU the same control sits under interest-based marketing.

## exitRoute.02.title
Find The Subscriptions

## exitRoute.02.body
Automatic payments hides years of recurring billing you have forgotten. Cancel from there before you touch anything else, or they will simply fail later.

## exitRoute.03.title
Move The Money Out

## exitRoute.03.body
Withdraw any balance to your bank and let it clear. Money sitting in a payment account is not protected the way a bank deposit is.

## exitRoute.04.title
Switch The Merchants

## exitRoute.04.body
Wherever PayPal is your saved method, change it to a card or direct debit. This is the slow part, and it is the part that actually frees you.

## exitRoute.05.title
Close, Once It Is Empty

## exitRoute.05.body
Closing with a balance, an open dispute or a pending payment will fail. Clear all three, then close the account from the settings page.
