## hero.standfirst
Spend a credit and you have not bought the book. You have licensed it, inside one app, from a shop that can <em>withdraw</em> it.

## subject.statement
The word for what Audible sells is not ownership. Authors are entised to lock in to be there, and listeners are locked in <em>afterwards</em>, and both conditions are deliberate.

## reason.01.title
I Won't Back That

## reason.01.body
Authors are bound and I am not comfortable that the catalogue I pay for is built on that exclusion.

## reason.02.title
I've Seen This Before

## reason.02.body
During 2026 Audible replaced its flat royalty with a pooled model paying by listening time. It is the arrangement music has spent a decade arguing about, and I would rather not watch books repeat it.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## alternatives.intro
One sells you the file outright and splits the money with a bookshop. The other is European, and <em>cheaper</em>.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Libro.fm

## alternative.01.locationLine
Audiobooks · Seattle

## alternative.01.country
United States

## alternative.01.blurb
An employee-owned certified B Corp selling six hundred thousand audiobooks with no rights management at all, sharing the profit with an independent bookshop you choose. Credits never <em>expire</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It is American, so the jurisdiction is no better than Audible's, and the monthly price is much the same. What changes is that the file is yours and a bookshop near you takes a cut.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
United States, Seattle. Inside US legal process, like the service you are leaving.

## alternative.01.ownership
Libro.fm, Inc., employee-owned and structured as a Social Purpose Corporation. Independently held, with no parent company.

## alternative.01.funding
Membership credits and one-off purchases, with profit shared with your chosen bookshop. No advertising.

## alternative.01.price
Around $14.99 a month for one credit, and credits can be bought without a membership.

## alternative.01.referralUrl
https://libro.fm/

## alternative.02.label

## alternative.02.name

## alternative.02.locationLine

## alternative.02.blurb
A Swedish service with eight hundred thousand titles across forty languages in more than twenty-five markets, priced well below the American options. European company, European <em>law</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
This is a subscription, so you are renting again and own nothing when you stop. It solves the jurisdiction and the price, and leaves the ownership problem exactly where it was.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
Sweden. An EU company, so GDPR and European consumer law apply directly.

## alternative.02.ownership
Storytel, Stockholm, publicly listed in Sweden. No American parent and no advertising business.

## alternative.02.funding
Subscriptions only, across more than twenty-five markets. It also publishes its own original titles.

## alternative.02.price
From around €6.49 a month, varying by country, with a free trial.

## alternative.02.referralUrl

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Spend The Credits First

## exitRoute.01.body
Unused credits vanish when the membership ends. Spend every one before you cancel, on the longest books you will actually finish.

## exitRoute.02.title
Check What You Own

## exitRoute.02.body
Open your library and count the titles. That list is the thing you are deciding whether to leave behind, and most people have never looked at it whole.

## exitRoute.03.title
Ask Your Library

## exitRoute.03.body
Public libraries lend audiobooks free through apps, and the author still gets paid. It is the cheapest step here and the one people most often forget.

## exitRoute.04.title
Buy The Next One Elsewhere

## exitRoute.04.body
Before cancelling, buy a single audiobook from a shop that gives you the file. Owning one is what makes the difference obvious.

## exitRoute.05.title
Cancel, Keep The Library

## exitRoute.05.body
Cancelling the membership leaves purchased titles in the account, so do not delete the Amazon account itself. Keep it dormant and empty instead.
