## hero.title
Leave Spotify

## hero.standfirst
This is not a privacy page. Spotify is European and behaves better than most, and the argument here is simply about where your ten pounds <em>lands</em>.

## subject.statement
Musicians have been leaving for two years while listeners stayed. The people who make the thing you are paying for have already <em>decided</em>, and that is worth a moment of your attention.

## subject.model
Subscriptions And Ads

## reason.01.title
Where The Money Goes

## reason.01.body
Daniel Ek's own investment firm led a six hundred million euro round into Helsing, a military AI company he now chairs. That is his money rather than Spotify's, and artists have left over it regardless.

## reason.02.title
Paid By Share, Not Play

## reason.02.body
Spotify says plainly that it pays by streamshare rather than per stream, so royalties come from a pool the largest catalogues dominate. Independent artists see roughly three to five tenths of a penny per play.

## reason.03.title
The Pool Keeps Filling

## reason.03.body
Thousands of AI-generated tracks and synthetic artists have arrived on the platform. Every one of them draws from the same pool of money as the musicians you actually meant to pay.

## alternatives.intro
Neither is cheaper, and that is the point. One pays musicians several times more per play; the other simply <em>sells</em> you the record.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Qobuz

## alternative.01.locationLine
Music Streaming · Paris

## alternative.01.blurb
A French service with a full catalogue, lossless audio, and a reported payout several times the market average. It also refuses AI-generated music outright, which no major rival <em>does</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
There is no free tier and it costs a little more than Spotify, so the switch is a real decision rather than a painless one. Its playlists and recommendations are also plainer, which some people miss badly.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
France. EU jurisdiction and data protection law apply.

## alternative.01.ownership
Qobuz Music SAS, Paris, founded 2007. Privately held by Xandrie SA rather than listed or investor-controlled.

## alternative.01.funding
Subscriptions and download sales. No advertising tier and no free plan.

## alternative.01.price
Around $12.99 a month for one person, with a family plan near $17.99.

## alternative.02.label
For Buying, Not Renting

## alternative.02.name
Bandcamp

## alternative.02.locationLine
Direct Sales · Oakland

## alternative.02.blurb
You buy the record and the artist keeps most of the money, typically over eighty per cent. The file is then yours, which no subscription anywhere can <em>promise</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
It is not a streaming service, so it replaces owning music rather than background listening. It was also sold to Epic Games and then to Songtradr in 2023, so the independence it is loved for is now a matter of ownership rather than structure.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
United States, California. Inside US legal process, unlike the first option.

## alternative.02.ownership
Owned by Songtradr, a private music licensing company, since 2023. Previously independent, then briefly owned by Epic Games.

## alternative.02.funding
A share of each sale, with the rest going to the artist or label. No advertising.

## alternative.02.price
You pay per album or track. Nothing recurring, and nothing to cancel.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Export The Playlists

## exitRoute.01.body
Use a transfer tool before you cancel, because playlists do not survive the account. An afternoon now saves a decade of collecting later.

## exitRoute.02.title
Buy Five Records

## exitRoute.02.body
Pick five albums you have played to death and buy them outright. That single act pays the artists more than your entire subscription history did.

## exitRoute.03.title
Keep One Month Overlapping

## exitRoute.03.body
Run the new service alongside for a month before cancelling. Catalogue gaps are real and you should find yours before you are committed.

## exitRoute.04.title
Tell Them Why

## exitRoute.04.body
Use the cancellation form to say what prompted it. Cancellations get counted; stated reasons get read, and the difference matters more than it should.

## exitRoute.05.title
Cancel, Then Delete

## exitRoute.05.body
Cancelling only stops the billing, leaving the account and its listening history intact. Close the account separately from the privacy settings.
