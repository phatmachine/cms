# Leaving You — Booking.com

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't take me places — you took me everywhere.

You know where I've slept for years. Who I travelled with. Which cities I keep going back to, what I'll pay for a view. The anniversary trip. The funeral I flew home for.

I trusted you to show me the world honestly. Then I looked at what you were selling.

You list rooms in Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank. The International Court of Justice has said those settlements break international law. The UN human rights office has named you, edition after edition, in its database of companies doing business there.

You saw it too. You added a warning telling travellers the area was "disputed, conflict-affected or high-risk." But a warning label is not a stance. You named the problem, and you kept taking the booking.

And you were never just Booking.com. You're Booking Holdings: the same company behind Priceline, Agoda, Kayak and OpenTable.

I let you plan my holidays, my work trips, my escapes. But I'm done booking with a company that knows where the line is and keeps selling rooms on the other side of it. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done waiting for you to grow a spine.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Hotels and hosts I book directly. Local agents who answer to the communities they serve. Travel that leaves my money with the people who live there.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
