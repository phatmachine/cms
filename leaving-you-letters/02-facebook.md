# Leaving You — Facebook

Status: Approved by Mark with his edit to the Cambridge Analytica paragraph, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't keep us close — for a while, you did.

You were where I found my old school friends. Where my aunt watched my kids grow up from the other side of the world. Where we shared the wedding photos, the birthday wishes, the condolences when Grandad died. You held my hometown, my family tree, and every photo I was ever tagged in.

I thought you were the place where my relationships lived. I didn't understand they were what you sold.

Then I learned how far it went. People took a personality quiz on your platform — and through them, data from millions of their friends was collected. People who never took the quiz. People like me. The US Federal Trade Commission fined you for breaking your promise to protect it.

And you were never just Facebook. You're Meta: Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads. One company holding my photos, my messages and my contacts under one roof.

I let you become my address book, my photo album, my town noticeboard. Leaving feels like moving out of my own neighbourhood. But I'm done turning the people I love into your inventory. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting you stand between me and my own family.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Group chats that stay between the people in them. Albums only my family can see. Places to gather with no ads to sell and no feed to game.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
