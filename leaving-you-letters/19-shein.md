# Leaving You — Shein

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you weren't fun — you were the most fun I ever had spending money.

You know my size in every country, everything I left in my cart, the colour I always pick, and the night I spent more than I meant to because the timer was running out.

I came for a bargain. You gave me a game. Spin the wheel. Collect the points. Beat the clock. So many new things every single day that there was always something new to want.

Then Europe's consumer authorities looked closely. Their findings described discounts that didn't reflect real earlier prices, deadlines that weren't real, and green claims that didn't hold up. The countdown was never counting down to anything.

And the clothes? Worn twice, then gone. A dress that costs less than a coffee isn't a bargain. It's tomorrow's landfill.

We did this together. Every parcel I opened was one less sale for the tailor down the road, the local label, the workshop that used to make things here. You didn't kill local production on your own. We did, one cart at a time.

You were never just a shop. You're a company headquartered in Singapore, built on factories in China, shipping straight to my door, past the shops on my own street.

But I'm done buying clothes made to be thrown away. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done playing a game where the prize is more stuff.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Second-hand shops and clothing swaps. Local makers who'll mend what they sold me. Fewer clothes, worn for years.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
