## hero.title
I'm Leaving YouTube

## hero.standfirst
A model now reads your viewing history to decide how old you are. If it decides wrongly, the way to argue is a <em>passport</em>.

## subject.statement
Your watch history stopped being a record and became evidence. YouTube reads it to reach a conclusion about who you are, and will not say which parts <em>counted</em>.

## subject.jurisdiction
USA

## subject.owner
Google LLC (Alphabet Inc.)

## subject.users
You

## subject.model
Ad Targeting, Opinion Influencing

## reason.01.title
It Guesses My Age

## reason.01.body
Since August 2025 YouTube has used a model to estimate whether American users are under eighteen, from watch history, searches and how old the account is. The birthday I entered is no longer what decides.

## reason.02.title
I'd Need A Passport

## reason.02.body
If the estimate is wrong, YouTube offers three ways to appeal: a government identity document, a credit card, or a photograph of my face. I am not willing to hand over more data to correct a guess.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## alternatives.intro
Nothing holds that catalogue, so both of these ask you to watch less and choose it. Neither runs ads in the part you pay for, and both <em>pay</em> the people who made what you watch.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
CuriosityStream

## alternative.01.locationLine
Documentaries · Silver Spring

## alternative.01.country
United States

## alternative.01.blurb
A documentary service for science, history and nature, with no advertising in the subscription and nothing to skip. Everything on it was commissioned or licensed, so the people who made it were <em>paid</em> before you pressed play.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It is not a replacement for YouTube and does not pretend to be — none of the channels you follow are there, and nothing imports. Its licensing arm, which sells footage to companies training AI models, now earns more than its subscriptions do.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
United States. A Maryland company inside US legal process, including the CLOUD Act.

## alternative.01.ownership
CuriosityStream Inc., publicly traded on NASDAQ as CURI. Founded by John Hendricks, who started the Discovery Channel.

## alternative.01.funding
Subscriptions, advertising on its free channels, and content licensing — which since early 2026 out-earns the subscriptions.

## alternative.01.price
$4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, ad-free. The separate Curiosity Now channel is free but carries advertising.

## alternative.02.blurb
The platform built by video makers who wanted out from under the algorithm, owned by the creators themselves and funded entirely by subscriptions. No advertising and no sponsor <em>reads</em>.

## alternative.02.catch
It is invitation-only and modest in size, so most channels you follow will not be there. CuriosityStream holds just under a sixth of it, so the two options on this page share a shareholder.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction

## alternative.02.ownership
Nebula Entertainment and Broadcasting LLC, owned by its creators through Standard, with CuriosityStream holding 16.875 percent.

## alternative.02.funding
Subscriptions only, shared with the creators whose work you watch. No advertising anywhere.

## alternative.02.price
Around $6 a month, or $60 a year, often discounted through a creator's link.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Read Your History

## exitRoute.01.body
Open your YouTube watch history and scroll back a year. That list is what the model is reading, and most people have never seen it whole.

## exitRoute.02.title
Turn The History Off

## exitRoute.02.body
At myactivity.google.com you can pause and delete YouTube history. It will degrade recommendations, which is rather the point of doing it.

## exitRoute.03.title
Export Your Subscriptions

## exitRoute.03.body
Google Takeout gives you your subscription list as a file. Nothing here imports it, but it is the only full record of what you actually watch.

## exitRoute.04.title
Pay Two Creators Directly

## exitRoute.04.body
Pick two channels you would miss and pay them where they keep it — a membership, a shop, a Patreon. Leaving quietly costs them more than it costs Google.

## exitRoute.05.title
Watch Somewhere Else First

## exitRoute.05.body
Spend a fortnight watching somewhere else before deciding anything. The habit is what binds you here, not the catalogue.
