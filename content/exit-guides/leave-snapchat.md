## reason.01.title
Parents See Names Only

## reason.01.body
Snap's own Family Center shows a parent which friends and groups a teen has chatted with in the last seven days, and not what was said. I hate being asked to parent from a list of names, and I would rather say so.

## reason.02.title
The Regulator Was Here

## reason.02.body
The FTC charged in 2014 that Snapchat's "disappear forever" promise was false, and it settled under a twenty-year order. In 2025 the FTC referred a complaint about My AI and young users to the Justice Department; a referral is not a finding.

## reason.03.title
The Courts Are Asking

## reason.03.body
Snap settled the first two cases set for trial in California's social media addiction proceeding, on confidential terms, and a judge let New Mexico's child-safety case proceed. These are allegations, not findings, and I hate what they describe.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Offer Somewhere Else

## exitRoute.01.body
Agree with your child where the conversations will move before you take anything away. For a younger child, install Kinzoo yourself and approve their contacts.

## exitRoute.02.title
Save Their Memories

## exitRoute.02.body
In Snapchat go to Settings, then My Data, and tick Export your Memories. Do it together, since it needs their login, and the zip arrives by email.

## exitRoute.03.title
Cancel The Bills

## exitRoute.03.body
Cancel Snapchat+ or any Memories storage plan in the app store you bought it through, and remove saved payment cards. Do it before deleting, so nothing bills afterwards.

## exitRoute.04.title
Keep A Net

## exitRoute.04.body
While they still use it, ask them to turn on Family Center so you can see who they message. Say plainly that you cannot see what is said, and why you are asking.

## exitRoute.05.title
Delete Together

## exitRoute.05.body
Check the export opens first. Then delete the account together, which opens the Snapchat accounts portal; it stays deactivated for 30 days, your window to reverse it.
