## hero.title
Leave Amazon

## hero.standfirst
It sold nearly seventy billion dollars of advertising last year. The search results you trust are an <em>auction</em>, and you are the prize.

## subject.statement
You think of it as a shop with adverts in it. The accounts say the opposite: an advertising business with a <em>warehouse</em> attached, where shelf position goes to whoever bids.

## subject.jurisdiction
USA

## subject.owner
Amazon.com, Inc.

## subject.users

## subject.model
Retail And Ads

## reason.01.title
The Shelf Is Sold

## reason.01.body
Amazon's advertising business approached seventy billion dollars in 2025, making it the third largest in the world. Those Sponsored Products sit in the search results you are reading as recommendations.

## reason.02.title
What Sellers Pay, You Pay

## reason.02.body
In August the FTC and twenty-two states alleged Amazon secretly inflated those ad auctions for seven years, extracting billions. Amazon rejects this and nothing is proven, but the regulator says the cost reached shoppers.

## reason.03.title
Kept As Long As Needed

## reason.03.body
Amazon's own privacy notice sets no retention period, only that it keeps your information as long as required. That record is shared across the group, from Ring to Whole Foods to Twitch.

## alternatives.intro
Nothing replaces a shop that sells everything, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. These two cover where most of the <em>money</em> actually goes.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Back Market

## alternative.01.locationLine
Refurbished Tech · Paris

## alternative.01.blurb
A French marketplace for professionally refurbished phones, laptops and appliances, sold with warranties by around eighteen hundred vetted refurbishers. A certified B Corp, so the mission sits in the <em>articles</em>.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It only sells technology, so it replaces one aisle rather than the shop. It has also raised more than a billion dollars from investors including Goldman Sachs and the Arnault family's vehicles, which is a lot of expectation to carry.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
France. EU consumer and data protection law applies to the marketplace.

## alternative.01.ownership
Back Market, Paris, founded 2014. Founders hold roughly a fifth; the rest sits with investors including Eurazeo, General Atlantic and Generation Investment Management.

## alternative.01.funding
Marketplace commission, plus an optional protection subscription. No advertising business attached.

## alternative.01.price
Free to buy from. An optional Plus tier runs about €3.99 a month for extended protections.

## alternative.02.label
For Everything Textile

## alternative.02.name
Vinted

## alternative.02.locationLine
Secondhand · Vilnius

## alternative.02.blurb
Europe's largest secondhand marketplace, Lithuanian owned and profitable, covering clothes, books, toys and homeware. Nothing new is manufactured for you, which makes it the <em>cheapest</em> option twice over.

## alternative.02.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
You are buying from strangers, so condition varies and returns are awkward compared with one-click retail. A buyer protection fee is added to every purchase, which is how the free listings get paid for.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
Lithuania. EU jurisdiction, with operations across twenty-six markets.

## alternative.02.ownership
Vinted Group UAB, Vilnius, founded 2008. Privately held by founders, staff and investors including Lightspeed and Accel.

## alternative.02.funding
Buyer protection fees and promoted listings. Profitable since 2023, on roughly €1.1 billion of revenue.

## alternative.02.price
Free to list and free to browse. A protection fee applies when you buy.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Turn Off Ad Targeting

## exitRoute.01.body
Go to amazon.com/adpreferences and switch off personalised advertising. It takes a minute and it works whether or not you ever leave.

## exitRoute.02.title
Read Your Own Order History

## exitRoute.02.body
Scroll back five years of orders. Most people find a surprising amount they did not need, which is a better argument than anything written here.

## exitRoute.03.title
Rescue What You Bought

## exitRoute.03.body
Kindle books, Audible titles and photo storage all live inside the account. Download or export them before you go anywhere near closing it.

## exitRoute.04.title
Break The Default

## exitRoute.04.body
Delete the app and the saved card, then buy the next three things somewhere else. The habit is the product, and it breaks in about a fortnight.

## exitRoute.05.title
Close It Or Cool It

## exitRoute.05.body
Closing the account forfeits Kindle, Prime Video and Alexa purchases permanently. For most people, an empty account with no card saved is the honest end point.
