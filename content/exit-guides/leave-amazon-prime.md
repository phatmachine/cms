## reason.01.title
That Name Told Me

## reason.01.body
Amazon staff called the cancellation flow “Iliad”, after the poem about a ten-year journey home. The name was internal and documented, and once I knew it I could not unsee what the design was for.

## reason.02.title
A Court Saw It

## reason.02.body
In September 2025 a federal judge found Amazon took billing details before disclosing what Prime cost and how to cancel. It settled three days into trial for two and a half billion dollars, and I stopped giving it the benefit of the doubt.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Check For Your Refund

## exitRoute.01.body
If you held Prime between June 2019 and June 2025, you may be owed up to fifty-one dollars. Some refunds were automatic; a claims process opened afterwards.

## exitRoute.02.title
Count What You Use

## exitRoute.02.body
List the Prime benefits you touched in the last year. Most people find two, against a hundred and thirty-nine dollars, and the list ends the argument.

## exitRoute.03.title
Rescue The Photos

## exitRoute.03.body
Prime includes unlimited full-resolution photo storage, and losing it is the thing people regret. Download everything before you cancel, not after.

## exitRoute.04.title
Cancel, And Expect Friction

## exitRoute.04.body
The flow is legally required to be simpler now. Go to Manage Membership and refuse every offer to pause, downgrade or take a discount instead.

## exitRoute.05.title
Let The Delivery Habit Die

## exitRoute.05.body
Without Prime, the minimum order threshold makes you batch purchases and wait. That pause is doing the real work, and it is worth more than the fee.
