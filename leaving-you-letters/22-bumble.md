# Leaving You — Bumble

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't try — you promised me someone.

You know who I swipe on and who I skip. My type. My deal-breakers. The photos I changed when nothing was working, the messages I sent at midnight that never got a reply. You know exactly how lonely I was, and when.

I came to meet a person. You gave me a game. Swipe, match, wait. The little rush when a match landed, and the empty feeling when it went nowhere.

And when I wanted to see who had actually liked me, there was a price. Premium, then a tier above Premium. Hope, in monthly instalments.

Then your billboards told me a vow of celibacy wasn't the answer, as if choosing to be alone was a problem only you could fix. You apologised and took them down. But the message had landed: being alone was something to fix, and you had a subscription for it.

You were never just a dating app. You're a business that earns more the longer I keep looking. An app that worked perfectly would lose me.

But I'm done paying monthly for hope. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done measuring my worth in matches.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Clubs, classes and teams where people meet for real. Friends of friends, around a table instead of a screen. Small, member-run events where nobody profits from me staying single.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
