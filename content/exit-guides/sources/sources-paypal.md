## Dropped for lack of source

- **The 2022 acceptable-use-policy $2,500 fine story.** PayPal said the policy update went out in error and withdrew it. Four years old and formally retracted; printing it would be the stale-scandal trap.
- **Account freezes and 180-day holds.** Widely complained about and probably the single biggest practical grievance against PayPal. Not researched this session, so not printed. Worth a proper pass — if it can be sourced to PayPal's own terms, it would make a stronger reason than any currently on the page.
- **"PayPal sells your data."** PayPal does not describe it as selling, and at least one assessment concludes it does not sell in the conventional sense. The page says "discloses", which is PayPal's own verb.
- **Currency conversion margins.** A real cost difference against Wise and a good argument, but not researched here.
- **The EuroPA alliance.** Wero signed a memorandum with Bizum, Bancomat, MB WAY and Vipps MobilePay in February 2026, together claiming ~130 million users across 13 countries. Genuinely relevant for readers in Spain, Italy, Portugal and the Nordics, where Wero itself is unavailable. Not printed because the interoperability is still being built, but it is the obvious addition when it goes live.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **`subject.users` is a TK.** This page cannot publish until the figure is filled from PayPal's latest results. Deliberately left unmistakable rather than guessed.
- **Reason 02's three-state quotation** is PayPal's own wording but was reached through a secondary site. Find it in the live privacy statement. It is the most quotable line on the page and the one most likely to be challenged.
- **The Wise buyer-protection catch** is reasoned rather than sourced. Confirm against Wise's terms, or soften to "does not offer PayPal's buyer protection".
- **Wero's member count.** Sources give 16 founding and 20 current. Confirm on epicompany.eu.
- **The PayPal Ads product.** The model field says "Fees And Ads". Confirm the advertising network exists as a named, current product rather than an announced intention.

## Goes stale

- **Wero.** The fastest-moving item here, and in a good direction. Luxembourg went live around June 2026, the Netherlands is migrating iDEAL across, e-commerce acceptance is arriving and the EuroPA tie-up targets 2027. Each of those improves this recommendation. Re-check quarterly — this is one of the few alternatives in the series getting better rather than more compromised.
- **Wise's listing.** Newly Nasdaq-listed. Watch whether the primary listing and centre of gravity move fully to the US, which would weaken the catch's framing or strengthen it, depending on your view.
- **PayPal's privacy statement.** Revised at least twice between November 2025 and January 2026 with the personalised-shopping wording intact. Re-check at each revision; all three reasons quote it.
