# Leaving You — Canva

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you weren't easy — you made me feel like a designer.

You hold my birthday invitations, my business cards, the flyer for the school fair, the pitch deck that won me my first client. You know the fonts I always reach for and the templates I keep coming back to.

I came because I couldn't afford a designer. Then I stopped thinking I needed one. So did everyone else.

Then the posters started to look the same. Same layouts, same fonts, same smiling stock faces, from the café on my street to the notice on the lamppost. The local designer who used to make our town look like *us* lost the work, one template at a time.

And once I depended on you, the price changed. You raised team prices by up to several times over, pointing to new AI features I hadn't asked for. You bought an AI image company, and the rival design tools the professionals used.

You were never just a design app. You're a company building AI to design for me, and calling it creativity.

But I'm done squeezing my ideas into someone else's template. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting a machine decide what my town looks like.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Free, open design tools I can keep using whatever happens. Files I can open anywhere. Local designers, paid to make things that look like us.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
