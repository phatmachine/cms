## Source-quality warning

**Most of the alternatives research came from a single publisher (onestowatch.com), across four near-identical SEO pages.** The per-stream comparison tables are internally consistent and match figures reported elsewhere, but this is affiliate-shaped content, not independent analysis, and it should not be the sole basis for a recommendation.

Before publishing, verify independently:
- Qobuz's payout figure (it is Qobuz's own published number — find Qobuz's statement)
- Qobuz's no-AI policy (find Qobuz's own announcement)
- Bandcamp's artist share (Bandcamp publishes this)

This is the thinnest sourcing behind any alternative in sixteen issues.

## Dropped for lack of source

- **ICE recruitment advertising on Spotify.** A genuine 2026 flashpoint, and Spotify defended the placements as part of a government campaign that did not violate its policies. Left out: it is US-specific, politically charged in a way the rest of the series is not, and the campaign had reportedly ended. It would also change the register of the page from economics to politics.
- **The 2026 class actions** over podcast licensing, family-plan eligibility, price-hike disclosures and songwriter royalties. Unresolved allegations, not researched individually.
- **Allegations that Spotify suppressed royalty increases before the Copyright Royalty Board.** Serious if true, sourced only to a summary, and squarely contested.
- **Joe Rogan and the 2022 Neil Young episode.** Four years old and thoroughly relitigated.
- **Ek's departure from the chief executive role.** One 2026 source refers to him as "former CEO". Not verified this session, and reason 01 is written to hold either way — he chairs Helsing regardless.
- **Any privacy or data claim.** None was found that meets this series' bar. That absence is itself the finding.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **The standfirst's concession must stay.** "This is not a privacy page" is what makes the rest of it honest. Spotify is the first subject in this series that does not deserve the usual framing.
- **The Helsing qualifier must stay.** Prima Materia is Ek's personal firm. A fact-check has already found advocacy on this topic distorting the link. Getting it wrong would hand Spotify an easy correction and discredit the page.
- **Add Spotify's AI crackdown to reason 03**, or verify what it does. Currently the page states the problem without the company's response, which is the fairness gap flagged on Uber in issue 015 and should not become a habit.
- **Confirm the Helsing round size** — sources give €600m and $700m.
- **All alternative figures need independent verification.** See the source-quality warning.

## Goes stale

- **The boycott.** Ongoing since 2025 and still growing. If a major label or a household-name artist leaves, reason 01 strengthens considerably.
- **Spotify's AI policy.** Moving quickly. Reason 03 could be overtaken within a quarter.
- **Bandcamp's ownership.** Sold twice in two years. A third sale would make the catch the lead of that alternative rather than a footnote.
- **Qobuz's independence.** Privately held by a single parent. Worth watching.
- **Ek's role.** Chairman of Helsing, and reportedly moved from chief executive at Spotify. Confirm both at republication.
