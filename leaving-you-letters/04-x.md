# Leaving You — X

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you were boring — you were never boring.

You were where I heard the news first. Where I argued with strangers at midnight and sometimes changed my mind. Where a small account could reply to a scientist, a president, a hero — and now and then be heard. I built my voice with you, one follower at a time, for years.

I thought you were a public square. I didn't understand a square could be bought.

Then you were. The rules changed under my feet. The blue tick that once meant "this is really them" went on sale, and Europe's regulators ruled that it deceived people and left them open to scams. My posts were switched on, by default, to train an AI. Then you were sold again, to that AI company, and folded into a rocket company. Whoever owns you decides what I see.

I let you become my newspaper, my debate hall, my megaphone. Leaving feels like going quiet. But I'm done shouting into a room someone else owns. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting whoever holds the keys decide what I hear.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Networks no single buyer can own. Feeds in the order things happened. Communities that set their own rules.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
