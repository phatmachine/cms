## reason.01.title
Very Likely Illegal

## reason.01.body
In May the Commission fined Temu two hundred million euros, finding EU shoppers are very likely to meet illegal items there. Its testing found chargers failing safety checks and baby toys with banned chemicals.

## reason.02.title
The Feed Amplifies It

## reason.02.body
The Commission also found Temu never assessed how its recommendations and paid influencers spread those products further. Separate proceedings into the app's addictive design remain open.

## reason.03.title
Four Countries, One Company

## reason.03.body
The parent is incorporated in the Cayman Islands, files from Dublin and is listed in New York. When filings first said Dublin, the company said its headquarters would always be Shanghai.

## alternatives.intro
The cheap thing that arrives in four days is the whole product, so nothing matches it. These two cost more and <em>last</em> longer.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Refurbed

## alternative.01.locationLine
Refurbished Goods · Vienna

## alternative.01.blurb
An Austrian marketplace for professionally restored electronics and appliances, every item carrying at least a twelve-month warranty after a forty-step check. A certified B Corp with a published <em>score</em>, not just a slogan.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
A refurbished kettle costs several times a new one from Temu, and that gap is the entire argument. The company also cut a fifth of its staff recently, which is worth knowing before you rely on its warranties.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Austria. EU consumer protection and product safety law applies to everything sold.

## alternative.01.ownership
Refurbed Marketplace GmbH, Vienna, founded 2017 by Peter Windischhofer, Kilian Kaminski and Jürgen Riedl. Venture backed.

## alternative.01.funding
Roughly ten per cent seller commission. Profitable since March 2025, with no advertising business.

## alternative.01.price
Free to buy from. You pay only for the item, which is usually well below new.

## alternative.02.label
For Everything Else

## alternative.02.name
Vinted

## alternative.02.locationLine
Secondhand · Vilnius

## alternative.02.blurb
Europe's biggest secondhand marketplace, covering clothes, homeware, books and toys. Nothing is manufactured to fill your order, so the safety question changes shape <em>entirely</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
You are buying from individuals, so there is no warranty and returns depend on goodwill. A buyer protection fee is added to every purchase, which is what pays for the free listings.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
Lithuania. EU jurisdiction, operating across twenty-six markets.

## alternative.02.ownership
Vinted Group UAB, Vilnius, founded 2008. Privately held by founders, staff and investors including Lightspeed and Accel.

## alternative.02.funding
Buyer protection fees and promoted listings. Profitable in 2025 on around €1.1 billion of revenue.

## alternative.02.price
Free to list and browse. A protection fee applies when you buy.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Check What Plugs In

## exitRoute.01.body
Find anything you bought there that charges, heats or goes near a child. Those are the categories the Commission actually tested, and the ones that failed.

## exitRoute.02.title
Kill The Notifications

## exitRoute.02.body
Turn off every push alert before you do anything else. The countdown timers and spinning wheels are the product, and they stop working once they are silent.

## exitRoute.03.title
Remove The Card

## exitRoute.03.body
Delete saved payment details from the account rather than just closing the app. Impulse buying needs stored card details, and it barely survives without them.

## exitRoute.04.title
Request Your Data

## exitRoute.04.body
Ask for a copy of your data and a deletion request through the app's privacy settings. EU and UK residents have the right; use it before the account goes.

## exitRoute.05.title
Delete The Account

## exitRoute.05.body
Deleting the app leaves the account and its order history intact. Close the account itself in settings, and expect a waiting period before it finalises.
