# Leaving You — Spotify

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because the music stopped — it never stopped. That was the point.

You know the song I play when I'm heartbroken, the playlist I run to, the album I played on repeat the summer everything changed. You turned my whole life into a soundtrack and handed it back to me every December, wrapped.

I came for the music. I thought I was paying the people who made it.

Then I learned how little reached them. A track on your platform has to pass a minimum number of plays every year before its artist earns anything at all. The small band I found at a pub. The local singer from the market. My subscription keeps flowing; it just flows past them.

And your founder took his fortune somewhere else. His investment firm led a funding round in Helsing, a company that builds AI for the battlefield and autonomous strike drones. Artists I love pulled their music off you in protest.

You were never just a music app. You're the company my subscription helped build, and the fortune it made for the man who started it.

I let you soundtrack my life. Leaving feels like going quiet. But I'm done renting music from a company that pays the artists last. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done helping build a fortune that backs war machines.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Music bought straight from the people who made it. Local radio and record shops. Platforms that pay for every play, from the very first one.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
