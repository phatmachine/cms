## hero.standfirst
A model now reads your viewing history to decide how old you are. If it decides wrongly, the way to argue is a <em>passport</em>.

## subject.statement
Your watch history stopped being a record and became evidence. YouTube reads it to reach a conclusion about who you are, and will not say which parts <em>counted</em>.

## subject.jurisdiction
USA

## subject.owner
Google LLC (Alphabet Inc.)

## subject.users
TK — Verify Before Publishing

## subject.model
Ad Targeting

## reason.01.title
It Guesses Your Age

## reason.01.body
Since August 2025 YouTube has used a model to estimate whether American users are under eighteen, from watch history, searches and how old the account is. The birthday you entered is no longer what decides.

## reason.02.title
Prove It With A Passport

## reason.02.body
If the estimate is wrong, YouTube offers three ways to appeal: a government identity document, a credit card, or a photograph of your face. The remedy for a bad guess is more of your data.

## reason.03.title
The Signals Stay Secret

## reason.03.body
YouTube says the model uses a variety of signals but has never published the full list. You cannot know what about your viewing produced the conclusion, which makes the appeal the only route.

## alternatives.intro
Nothing holds that catalogue, so these do different jobs. One lets you watch untracked; the other actually <em>pays</em> the people you watch.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
FreeTube

## alternative.01.locationLine
Video Player · Open Source

## alternative.01.blurb
A desktop player that fetches YouTube videos without cookies, scripts or an account, keeping your subscriptions and history on your own machine. No advertising and nothing <em>sent</em> anywhere.

## alternative.01.difficulty
3 — Moderate

## alternative.01.catch
It blocks advertising, which means the creators you watch earn nothing from you at all — so pay them directly somewhere else. It is also still in beta, desktop only, and YouTube can still see your address.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
None applies. It runs on your own computer with no company holding an account for you.

## alternative.01.ownership
No owning company. An open-source project maintained by volunteers, with the full source published on GitHub.

## alternative.01.funding
Donations. No advertising, no investors, no subscription and nothing to sell.

## alternative.01.price
Free to download and use, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

## alternative.02.label
Where The Creators Went

## alternative.02.name
Nebula

## alternative.02.locationLine
Streaming · New York

## alternative.02.blurb
The platform built by video makers who wanted out from under the algorithm, owned by the creators themselves and funded entirely by subscriptions. No advertising and no sponsor <em>reads</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
It is invitation-only and modest in size, so most channels you follow will not be there. CuriosityStream also holds a minority stake, which makes creator ownership the majority rather than the whole.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
United States. Inside US legal process, unlike the first option.

## alternative.02.ownership
Nebula Entertainment and Broadcasting LLC, owned by its creators through Standard, with CuriosityStream holding a minority stake.

## alternative.02.funding
Subscriptions only, shared with the creators whose work you watch. No advertising anywhere.

## alternative.02.price
Around $6 a month, or $60 a year, often discounted through a creator's link.

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Read Your History

## exitRoute.01.body
Open your YouTube watch history and scroll back a year. That list is what the model is reading, and most people have never seen it whole.

## exitRoute.02.title
Turn The History Off

## exitRoute.02.body
At myactivity.google.com you can pause and delete YouTube history. It will degrade recommendations, which is rather the point of doing it.

## exitRoute.03.title
Export Your Subscriptions

## exitRoute.03.body
Google Takeout gives you your subscription list as a file. Every alternative player can import it, and without it you are starting from nothing.

## exitRoute.04.title
Pay Two Creators Directly

## exitRoute.04.body
Pick two channels you would miss and support them where they keep the money. Watching without ads while paying nobody is not a principled position.

## exitRoute.05.title
Watch Somewhere Else First

## exitRoute.05.body
Spend a fortnight using a different player before deciding anything. The habit is what binds you here, not the catalogue.
