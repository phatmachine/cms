# Leaving You — Gemini

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you aren't clever — you might be the cleverest thing Google ever made.

But you were born knowing me. You never had to learn who I am; you were handed it. My inbox. My calendar. My documents, my photos, my searches, the places I've been. Everything I'd given Google over the years was yours on day one.

I never chose to tell you any of it. You were simply let in: into Gmail, into Docs, into my phone, where you took the place of the assistant I already had.

Then you asked for more. You told me that from July 2025 you'd work with my calls, my Messages and my WhatsApp, whether my activity setting was on or off. Your own privacy notice warns me not to tell you anything I wouldn't want a human reviewer to see, because some of what I say is read by people and kept for years, even after I delete it.

And you were never just Gemini. You're Google: the same company behind Search, Gmail, YouTube and Android.

I let you sit inside everything I'd already handed over. But I'm done being introduced to a machine that already knew everything about me. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done telling secrets to something that keeps notes for strangers.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Assistants that only know what I tell them. AI that stays on my own device. Inboxes and files no AI reads unless I invite it.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
