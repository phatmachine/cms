## hero.title
I'm Leaving Xbox

## hero.standfirst
Microsoft raised Game Pass by half in one go, crashed its own cancellation page, lost millions of subscribers, and quietly put it <em>back</em>.

## subject.statement
A library you rent is not a library. Everything on Game Pass can be withdrawn, repriced or removed, and 2025 was the year Microsoft <em>demonstrated</em> all three.

## reason.01.title
I'm Only Renting

## reason.01.body
Nothing in Game Pass becomes mine, however long I subscribe. Titles leave the catalogue without much warning, including the large ones I joined in order to play, and I am tired of building on rented ground.

## reason.02.title
They Tested My Limit

## reason.02.body
In October 2025 Ultimate went from twenty to thirty dollars, and so many people cancelled that the cancellation page fell over. Microsoft's own new chief later admitted it had cost them millions of subscribers, and I was one of them.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## alternatives.intro
Neither plays your console discs, and both are PC. What they offer instead is the thing a subscription cannot: you <em>keep</em> it.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
GOG

## alternative.01.locationLine
Games · Warsaw

## alternative.01.country
Poland

## alternative.01.blurb
A Polish store selling games with no rights management at all, downloadable as offline installers you keep forever. Its preservation programme repairs old titles so they still <em>run</em> today.

## alternative.01.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.01.catch
It is PC only, and the catalogue is classics and indies rather than the new console releases you are leaving behind. It also changed hands in December 2025, and preservation work costs more than it earns.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Poland. An EU company, with GDPR applying and the account data held there.

## alternative.01.ownership
Bought outright in December 2025 by Michał Kiciński, who co-founded it, for twenty-five million dollars. Independent of CD Projekt since.

## alternative.01.funding
Game sales, plus voluntary Patron donations that fund the preservation work. No advertising.

## alternative.01.price
You pay per game and keep the installer. There is nothing recurring.

## alternative.01.referralUrl
https://www.gog.com/en/about_gog

## alternative.02.label
Where Small Games Live

## alternative.02.name
itch.io

## alternative.02.locationLine
Indie Games · Independent

## alternative.02.country
United States

## alternative.02.blurb
A marketplace of over a million games where developers set what cut the platform takes, including nothing at all, and you can pay above the asking price if you want. Still owned by the person who <em>built</em> it.

## alternative.02.difficulty
2 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
This is experimental and independent work, not the blockbusters — a wonderful place to browse and a poor substitute for a console. Much of it is free, which is also why the platform runs on very little.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
United States. Inside US legal process, unlike the first option.

## alternative.02.ownership
Owned by its founder, Leaf Corcoran, since 2013. No investors, no parent company and no acquisition.

## alternative.02.funding
A ten per cent default cut of sales, which developers may set lower or waive entirely.

## alternative.02.price
Pay what you like above whatever the developer sets, often nothing at all.

## alternative.02.referralUrl
https://itch.io/

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Count The Months

## exitRoute.01.body
Add up what you have paid into Game Pass and list what you still have from it. The answer is nothing, and that arithmetic does the arguing.

## exitRoute.02.title
Finish The One Game

## exitRoute.02.body
Pick the single title keeping you subscribed and finish it this month. Subscriptions survive on the one thing you have not got round to.

## exitRoute.03.title
Buy It, Do Not Borrow It

## exitRoute.03.body
Take the game you have replayed most and buy a copy you own outright. One purchase makes the difference between renting and keeping obvious.

## exitRoute.04.title
Turn Off Auto-Renew

## exitRoute.04.body
Cancel the recurring payment in your Microsoft account rather than the console. Leave the remaining time to run out on its own.

## exitRoute.05.title
Keep The Console

## exitRoute.05.body
The discs and downloads you bought still work without a subscription. This is about leaving the rental, not throwing away hardware that still plays.
