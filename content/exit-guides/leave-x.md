## hero.title
I'm Leaving X

## hero.standfirst
Europe fined X one hundred and twenty million euros in December, and the first finding was that its blue ticks mean <em>nothing</em> at all.

## subject.statement
A public square only works if you can see who is speaking and who paid to be heard. X sells the first and obscures the second, and has now been <em>fined</em> for both.

## subject.jurisdiction

## subject.model
Ads And Subscriptions

## reason.01.title
Verified Meant Paid

## reason.01.body
The European Commission found that selling blue checkmarks deceives people, because nothing about the account has actually been verified. It called the result greater exposure to impersonation and scams, and I stopped trusting what I was seeing.

## reason.02.title
I Can't Check It

## reason.02.body
The same decision penalised an ad repository missing the content and the payer, and terms that forbid researchers from collecting public posts. Nobody outside the company can check what it amplifies, and that includes me.

## reason.03.title
American Rules & American Control

## reason.03.body
According to the World Economic Forum, the majority of data in the Western world is stored on U.S.-owned servers. Even using a US based digital application in a foreign country is bound by US legislation - local legislation and privacy controls do not protect you

## alternatives.intro
One is owned by nobody and cannot be sold. The other is far easier to join, and has <em>investors</em> who will eventually want paying.

## alternative.01.label
Recommended First Move

## alternative.01.name
Mastodon

## alternative.01.locationLine
Microblogging · Federated

## alternative.01.blurb
A network of independent servers speaking one protocol, with no company above them, no advertising, and a timeline in plain date order. Nobody owns the whole thing, so nobody can <em>sell</em> it.

## alternative.01.difficulty
3 — Moderate

## alternative.01.catch
You have to choose a server before you can start, which is the step that defeats most people. Its promised move to a European non-profit, announced in January 2025, is still not finished.

## alternative.01.jurisdiction
Depends on the server you join. Operations run from Europe, with a Belgian non-profit intended as the eventual home.

## alternative.01.ownership
No single owner. Operations run through Mastodon gGmbH, which lost its German charitable status, while a US non-profit holds the trademark pending transfer.

## alternative.01.funding
Donations and sponsorships. No advertising, no venture capital, and nothing for anyone to acquire.

## alternative.01.price
Free. Most servers ask for a contribution toward hosting.

## alternative.01.referralUrl
https://joinmastodon.org/

## alternative.02.label
The Softer Landing

## alternative.02.name
Bluesky

## alternative.02.locationLine
Microblogging · Delaware

## alternative.02.country
United States

## alternative.02.blurb
The easiest move if you are leaving this week: a familiar feed, people you know already there, and moderation filters you set yourself. Built on an open protocol, so your handle stays <em>yours</em>.

## alternative.02.difficulty
1 — Easy

## alternative.02.catch
It is a venture-backed American company that has raised well over a hundred million dollars and still has no revenue model. Nearly everyone uses Bluesky's own servers, so the decentralisation remains mostly theoretical.

## alternative.02.jurisdiction
United States. A Delaware public benefit corporation, and therefore inside US legal process.

## alternative.02.ownership
Bluesky Social, PBC. Held by staff and investors including Blockchain Capital and Bain Capital; its founding chief executive stepped back in March 2026.

## alternative.02.funding
Venture capital, with no advertising so far. How it will eventually make money is an open question.

## alternative.02.price
Free to use, with no advertising at present.

## alternative.02.referralUrl
https://bsky.app/

## exitRoute.heading
Five Steps / Out The Door

## exitRoute.01.title
Request The Archive

## exitRoute.01.body
Ask for your full data archive first and wait for the email. Years of posts, images and direct messages, in one file, before you touch anything else.

## exitRoute.02.title
Turn Off The Training

## exitRoute.02.body
In Settings, under Privacy and safety, find the Grok section and withdraw permission to use your posts. Do this even if you plan to stay a while.

## exitRoute.03.title
Take Your Following List

## exitRoute.03.body
The accounts you follow are the only thing worth carrying over. Tools exist to find the same people elsewhere, and most of them left before you did.

## exitRoute.04.title
Pin The Forwarding Address

## exitRoute.04.body
Post your new handle and pin it to your profile. Then stop posting here, because a live account keeps the feed worth farming.

## exitRoute.05.title
Deactivate, Then Delete

## exitRoute.05.body
Deactivation starts a thirty-day clock before the account is removed. Log back in during that window and the clock resets, so log out and leave it.
