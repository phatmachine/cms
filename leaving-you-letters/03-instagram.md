# Leaving You — Instagram

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you aren't beautiful — you're the most beautiful thing on my phone.

You've seen every version of me I wanted the world to believe. The holiday from its best angle. The dinner before anyone ate. The tenth take of a laugh. You know how long I look at my own face, how often I check who watched my story, which posts I deleted because they didn't do well enough.

I came to share my life with my friends. Somewhere along the way, I started living it for you. Sunsets became content. Birthdays became posts. I'd feel a moment and reach for my phone in the same breath.

And you knew what that cost. Your own researchers told you Instagram made body image worse for some teenage girls. You tested hiding the likes — then kept them on, and made switching them off my job.

You were never just Instagram. You're Meta: the same company behind Facebook, Messenger and WhatsApp.

I've performed for you so long that putting the phone down feels like disappearing. But I'm done measuring my worth in hearts. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done editing my life to fit your feed.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Photos shared only with the people who were there. Feeds that end. Places with no counts to chase.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
