## Dropped for lack of source

- **"TikTok is Chinese spyware" / the CCP-access framing.** The documented finding is narrower and stronger: EEA data was remotely accessible from China without equivalent protection, TikTok could not demonstrate otherwise, and it gave the regulator inaccurate information. The DPC decision explicitly turned on TikTok's failure to assess Chinese anti-terrorism and counter-espionage law — that is the sourced version, and it is what the page uses. Speculation about actual government access is not printed.
- **The US divest-or-ban politics.** Real, well documented, and irrelevant to a reader deciding whether to keep the app. The page treats the joint venture as an ownership fact, not a geopolitical story.
- **TikTok's effects on attention or teen mental health.** Genuinely contested and would need its own research pass. Also, the same argument would apply to the alternatives, which makes it a bad reason for an exit guide.
- **Douyin comparisons.** Douyin is a separate platform with roughly 755–766 million users. Interesting, not relevant to a reader leaving TikTok.
- **TikTok Shop and commerce data.** Not researched. Possibly a strong future reason.

## Weak link — fix before publishing

- **The user count.** No company disclosure exists, so this is the weakest metadata figure since issue 004. Estimates run 1.9–1.99bn. Either accept the estimate and label it as such in the CMS, or use the EU figure of roughly 169–175 million, which at least traces to regulatory filings.
- **The appeal.** TikTok said it would appeal the €530m decision in full. If that appeal has succeeded or is pending, reason 01 should say so. Check before publishing — this is the most likely correction request the page will receive.
- **PeerTube's current status.** Framasoft's authorship is established from earlier research, but PeerTube's own release cadence and health were not verified this session. Issue 004 already flagged Framasoft stepping back from Mobilizon; confirm PeerTube is not in the same position.
- **The `subject.jurisdiction` split.** Confirm the Payload field accepts it before this imports.

## Goes stale

- **The US joint venture.** Nine months old and the most likely thing here to move. Watch for changes to ByteDance's 19.9%, the board composition, or the algorithm-retraining arrangement.
- **Loops.** The fastest-improving item: App Store launch in January 2026, out of beta at some point. If it grows, the catch softens considerably and this becomes a much better recommendation. Re-check quarterly. It also inherits the single-maintainer risk flagged in issue 003 — Supernault runs both Loops and Pixelfed, so a problem there affects two issues.
- **The DPC compliance order.** TikTok was given six months from May 2025 to comply or face suspension of transfers to China. What happened at that deadline is not established here and is worth knowing.
- **Global user count.** Re-check annually; there is no authoritative source, so pick one tracker and stay with it rather than switching between estimates.
