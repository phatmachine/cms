# Leaving You — AliExpress

Status: Approved by Mark, 5 October 2026

---

I'm leaving you.

Not because you didn't have it — you had everything. Every gadget, every spare part, every strange little thing I didn't know I needed.

You know my address, my card, my late-night impulse buys, and the phone case I ordered three times because the first two broke.

I came because it was cheap. I told myself cheap was harmless.

Then Europe's regulators looked at what you were selling. Their preliminary finding said you hadn't done enough to stop illegal products spreading across your platform, unlicensed medicines among them, and had put too few resources into checking. You promised to do better. I'm still waiting to see it.

And I never really knew what I was holding. A charger with no safety mark. A toy with a label I couldn't read. A supplement making promises no one had checked.

You were never just a marketplace. You're Alibaba: a company answerable to Chinese law, holding my address and my payment details.

But I'm done gambling on what arrives in the post. I'm done pretending this relationship is fair. I'm done letting cheap decide what's safe enough for my home.

There are alternatives — built by people who don't think love means surveillance. Local shops that stand behind what they sell. Products with safety marks I can trust. Things built to last, and repairs instead of replacements.

I've given you enough. Now, I will give you no more.

It's time for me to leave.
